## Contributions and feedback

The content of this repository is generated and published automatically. Direct community contributions to the repository are not supported; therefore, Issues and Pull Requests cannot be submitted.

If you would like to report an error, provide feedback, or suggest a change regarding the content of this repository, please contact the National Tax and Customs Administration of Hungary (NAV) through its official contact form:
[Contact the National Tax and Customs Administration of Hungary](https://nav.gov.hu/ugyfeliranytu/keressen_minket/levelkuldes?subjectid=92).

Please clearly identify the affected repository and, where applicable, the affected form template and its version in your message.
